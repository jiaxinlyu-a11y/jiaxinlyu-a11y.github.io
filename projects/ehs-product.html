Designing the processes of a standard EHS product

An EHS system manages safety, emergency, equipment, training and environmental work in a factory. After benchmarking the market, I designed the processes for five areas of a standard product: over 100 prototype pages, with flow charts, status rules and role lanes.

01Starting point

I compared five vendors and three working systems against one function list, to see where ours was covered, needed work or was missing.

Together they showed what a standard product needs: A’s depth, C’s modular structure, and none of the repeated entries, pages or fields.
