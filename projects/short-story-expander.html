Short Story Expander

I distilled my own reading experience into writing skills, then built a local workbench around them. I decide the plot; the skills and the tool turn each planned beat into prose with an AI assistant, and a short story now takes about a third of the time.

01The problem

A short story used to take me about six hours to write, and often some small edits afterwards. I wanted to keep deciding the plot myself and speed up the part that turns each planned beat into prose, without losing my own taste.

02Building the skills, then the tool

The skills came first. They come from my own reading experience and from good stories I studied closely. I wrote down what makes them work as rules, then refined every rule slowly: write, read the result as a reader would, adjust one point, and write again.

The workbench then applies the skills. For a chosen block, it builds one task file from my rules, the characters and the previous passage, and the AI assistant writes inside that block only.

03What it changed

A story that took about six hours now takes about two, with small edits afterwards. Both times are my own estimates. The numbers below are for a story written this way and published on Fanqie Novel.
